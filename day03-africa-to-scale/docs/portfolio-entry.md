# Africa, to Scale
Day 03 / Polygons · 2026 MUSA 30-Day Map Challenge

A six-panel continent comparison centered on Africa. Real outlines are represented at one physical area scale, then rotated and repositioned without resizing. The final panel reverses the perspective, placing Africa over country-colored Asia and quantifying covered country portions.

Natural Earth geometry, WGS84 ellipsoidal areas and locally centered equal-area projections support a reproducible Python workflow. The map distinguishes relative area from literal containment, retaining every measured protrusion. Saved placements, source checksums and numerical audits accompany the high-resolution exports.
