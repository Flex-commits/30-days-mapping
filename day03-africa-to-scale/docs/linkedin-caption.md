Day 3: Polygons — Africa, to Scale.

I wanted to compare Africa with continents instead of the usual country puzzle. Each outline keeps its calculated area as it moves and rotates. The last panel flips the comparison: Africa over Asia, with the countries showing what lies beneath it.

Swipe through the individual panels for the area comparisons. Remote islands stay visible in full-extent context insets, while the main views focus on the larger landmasses.

One thing the geometry makes clear: having a smaller area doesn’t mean an outline will fit neatly inside another. I kept those edges visible.
