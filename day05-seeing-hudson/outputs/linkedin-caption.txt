#30DayMapChallenge — Day 5: Sight

Seeing the Hudson.
[Two shores. One river. Different views.]

A river can give you a skyline, a sunset, or a bridge framed by trees. Where you stand changes the picture… and so does when you look.

I paired six public places on the New York and New Jersey shores, using aerial maps and credited, dated photographs. The dashed lines connect the stories—not verified sightlines.
