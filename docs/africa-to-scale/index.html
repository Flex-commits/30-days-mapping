Area and shape tell different stories

A smaller area does not guarantee that one intact outline will nest inside another. Australia is fully contained in its saved placement. The others retain visible protrusions: the map reports the measured share inside and beyond the host. It makes no claim that every continent literally fits inside Africa.

Geography and method

Natural Earth 1:50m supplies the outlines. Each continent uses a locally centered WGS84 equal-area representation, normalized minutely to its ellipsoidal surface area. After preparation, only rotation and translation are used. No display simplification or clipping is applied.

Africa includes Madagascar and the saved regional island grouping. Australia includes Tasmania, rather than all Oceania. Europe and Asia use an explicit saved division through transcontinental countries. Antarctica includes historical perennial ice shelves, excluding seasonal sea ice. Enclosed interior rings are filled consistently; the measure is polygon surface area, not dry-land area.

These local projections support an area comparison; moving the shapes does not produce exact geographic relocation on the globe. Generalized boundaries and the historic Antarctic extent limit geographic precision.
