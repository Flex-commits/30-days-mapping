What is documented?

Modern Copernicus terrain and approximate OpenStreetMap landmarks. “Today’s streets” shows the present-day road extract, not ancient streets or a complete model of today’s buildings.

Blocks, markets, towers, foundations, people and clothing are design interpretations. Two stelae are schematic; their shapes and fallen orientation are not surveyed replicas. Heights are not vertically exaggerated.
