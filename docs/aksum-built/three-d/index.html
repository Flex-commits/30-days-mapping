What is documented?

Modern Copernicus terrain and approximate OpenStreetMap landmarks. “Today’s streets” shows the present-day road extract, not ancient streets or a complete model of today’s buildings.

Blocks, markets, towers, proposed foundations, shade trees, people and clothing are design interpretations. The church footprints are sourced; their elevations are photo-informed approximations. The stelae have rounded profiles and carved motifs, but are not scans; the fallen orientation is schematic. The older church and the 1965 cathedral are distinct models. Heights are not vertically exaggerated.

Inspired by evidence-led digital heritage work. This prototype is not a reconstruction of any historical date.
