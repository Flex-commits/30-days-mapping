# Aksum, Built — final claim review

Reviewed November 9, 2026. This completes the existing Utopia draft rather than inventing a new project.

| Claim / input | Decision | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| The Yeha reconstruction uses a 6 m podium and 4.32 m floor module; 8 storeys give 40.56 m. | Confirmed as the 2023 model’s dimensions, not every ancient building’s dimensions. | Drieschner et al. 2023, geometric-model section, linked in README. |
| A 2026 study establishes what every Aksum site could safely carry. | Rejected. Two wall-section studies at a different, earlier site cannot certify whole buildings or different foundations, materials, lateral loads or construction. | Scope of the study described by BTU, September 25, 2026. |
| “At least 19 storeys” is a universal maximum of 19. | Rejected. A reported modeled capacity is neither a universal maximum nor evidence of historical height. | BTU research report. Full journal article was not consulted; the institutional report is the cited source. |
| The illustrated 19-storey tower is about 88 m. | Confirmed arithmetic for this scenario only: 6 + 19 × 4.32 = 88.08. Not a safety or archaeological finding. | Saved config and independently checked calculations. |
| 13 / 10 / 9 storeys precisely match the stelae’s carved floors. | Not independently established here; removed as a factual claim. They remain declared artistic choices inspired by the forms. | Prior draft used photographic counts with varying published definitions. |
| Every place is real. | Too broad: house locations, footprints and twin-tower offsets are invented. Replaced with “Documented landmarks. Imagined towers and homes.” | OSM records versus saved scenario-building table. |
| The terrain is reconstructed ancient ground. | Rejected. It is a modern digital surface model, with surface objects and unresolved local acquisition date. | Copernicus source GeoTIFF metadata. |
| The original terrain crop is correctly located. | Confirmed independently from GeoTIFF pixel-center coordinates. Original crop elevation array matches exactly; window row 3079, column 2529, 80 × 109 samples. | `terrain-provenance.json`; source hash and transform. |
| Map linework represents contours. | Rejected. It consists of parallel sampled terrain profiles. | Render algorithm. |
| Great Temple survives to 14 m / Great Stele roughly 33 m long. | Confirmed; only these source measurements appear in the inset. | Ethiopia’s UNESCO tentative-list Yeha description and UNESCO Aksum description, linked in README. |
| Exact ancient populations, coinage dates and universal maximum house heights. | Omitted from this final map because they are unnecessary to its Utopia question and were not independently resolved. | Prior research included conflicting estimates. |
| Source geography establishes engineering feasibility. | Rejected. Positions and elevations support the drawing, not foundation, seismic, fire or construction assessments. | Method scope. |

Aksum and Yeha are different places and periods. The comparison is a design inspiration, not a combined reconstruction. The scenario is not a proposal to alter protected heritage sites. Existing earlier projects are retained.

Design retained the cream paper, serif lettering, vermilion towers and north-facing terrain profiles. Refinements add an Africa locator, UTM plan coordinates, checked source-grid georeferencing, a separate skyline detail and a simpler explanation of measured inputs versus imagined forms. Unsupported capacity language and broad historical-population claims were removed.
