**Documented landmarks. An imagined skyline.**

For Utopia, I imagined towers and homes on a sourced surface model of Aksum, Ethiopia. Landmark positions anchor the drawing; every building is a design scenario. Yeha reconstruction dimensions supply an illustrative height module, without implying historical Aksum heights or site-specific structural feasibility.
