#30DayMapChallenge — Day 6: Vintage

The River Remembers…

A river moves. But it doesn’t erase every trace.

Around Helena, Arkansas, the Mississippi’s old curves remain in lakes beside the channel. For this map, I paired USGS water geometry with details from a 1938 Mississippi River Commission survey.

[Old bends. A different path.]

I tried a different look this time… warm paper, ink, and a little calligraphy.
